HOW TO PLAY

Bring the right parts together.

Drag a number onto the number it combines with. For a power, drag the small exponent onto its base. You can also tap one number, then tap the other.

Any independent valid operation can be chosen. Enter its value, then the pair bloops into its result. Other pairs simply won’t combine. A copy of every completed step flies into your steps list.

G Grouping defines which parts belong together.
E Exponents.
M · D Multiplication and division, left to right.
A · S Addition and subtraction, left to right when the operations depend on one another.

Grouping lights up together with the operation you can do inside it. A fraction bar also groups its entire numerator and denominator. Simplify those parts before dividing.

Use Division display to switch between ÷ and stacked fractions. Use Multiplication display to choose ×, ·, or parentheses. Both choices also apply to your steps. To enter a fraction such as two times (3 + 4), all over 7, type (2(3+4))/7. Use parentheses around a numerator or denominator with several terms.

Choose a level and press New Expression, or enter your own. Show Me How replays the expression from the beginning using the same movements. Timer counts up; tap it again to pause.
